# Route lighting connections below the base

For floor-standing furniture with a service space beneath its base deck, route
the selected recessed lights' low-voltage leads into that space by default.
Keep connections accessible behind a removable kickboard. This is a coordinated
machining and purchased-component requirement, not a completed electrical design.

## One placement authority

- Derive the feed exit from the saved light run and the selected product's cable
  exit. Transform that point through the actual host and assembly frames.
- Locate the base passage directly beneath the groove/feed route. Transform the
  same route into each intersected part's local frame; never independently type
  hole positions into the cabinet floor and base deck.
- Include every intervening board. A hole in the deck alone is insufficient if
  the cabinet floor or the uncut end of a side panel still blocks the cable.
- Extend or connect the groove to the passage using a supported installation
  detail. Do not extend the luminaire body or invent a sharp cable bend to hide
  an unresolved transition. Roof/sloping runs need their own connected lead path.

## Size for the purchased components

The groove dimensions come from the exact selected luminaire profile. The cable
passage comes from the largest plug/lead component that must pass through during
the stated assembly sequence, with a recorded fit allowance. Do not equate groove
width with passage diameter. Include a selected grommet's bore when applicable.
Do not assume plugs can be cut off or disassembled.

The APEX 84 instruction sheet confirms a groove width of 4 mm (+0.3/-0) and depth
of 8 mm (+0.5/-0), and depicts a Micro24 lead. These are groove tolerances, not
cable-hole dimensions. Its drawing does not dimension that plug. Obtain a
dimensioned matching connector or measured sample before releasing its passage
as fabrication-ready; record any proposed clearance separately as provisional.

Source: [APEX 84 installation sheet, page 1](https://www.domusline.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/3493200_r1_APEX-84_instruction_sheet.pdf).

## Build and verify

- Each crossed part owns its actual through-cut and machining operation. Drill
  from its existing selected CNC face wherever possible; follow the shared
  one-face policy rather than adding a second setup by default.
- Check route continuity, connector insertion space and cable bend clearance,
  retained material, and conflicts with leg plates, screws and Cabineo features.
- Verify the removed material against the cutters' intersection with each actual
  part, accounting for overlapping existing cuts. A viewer marker is not a hole.
- Include leads, extensions, distribution and selected controls in the lighting
  BOM. Confirm reach along the routed path, not straight-line distance alone.
- Removing lighting removes its exclusive cable cuts and purchases on rebuild;
  preserve shared cable services still used by another feature.
- Record accessible connection space under the base. A selected driver must fit
  its manufacturer's mounting/clearance requirements; do not infer that all power
  equipment fits there merely because low-voltage connections do.

Keep unselected connectors, supply, cable reach or installation details visible
as open requirements. Never mark the installation complete from groove fit alone.
