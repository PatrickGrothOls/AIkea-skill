# Changelog

## 0.1.0-desktop.3 - 2026-09-23

- Carry saved material choices into generated panel specifications and parts lists.
- Correct CAD panel metalness and reject empty Blender lighting bakes.
- Preserve large mesh indices and complete hinge identities in open-door views.
- Add a hanging-rail installation module with manufacturer drawing datums.
- Require the documented Hettich CAD download and import verification workflow.

Exact hardware CAD and construction checks remain project-specific requirements.
The SL 322 support preview is explicitly identified until its CAD is imported.

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 - 2026-09-06

- Package all nine AIkea skills with local Codex and Claude discovery links.
- Include deterministic cabinet, base, door, drawer, and lighting builders and
  an interactive review viewer.
- Build the Cabineo machining cutter directly, preserving the custom brass
  threaded-insert receiver at diameter 9.1 mm and depth 12.5 mm.
- Include bundled viewer license notices and reproducible build checks.
- Document setup, a first-project walkthrough, platform support, and manual
  acquisition of exact manufacturer CAD.

This alpha supports design and review. Generated parts still lack the material
identity required by the fabrication gate; the complete manufacturing pack and
fresh end-to-end fabrication proof remain unfinished. Longer KA 4532 spacer
fixing screws and cabinet pilot specifications also remain unresolved.
