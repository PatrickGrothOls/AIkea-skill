# Hettich KA 4532 with spacer profile 13952

Use this reference for the approved 500 mm KA 4532 Silent System runner mounted
on the Hettich spacer profile. The spacer is a purchased component on both
cabinet sides, not a wooden packing strip generated by AIkea.

## Exact purchased set

- Runner pair: Hettich KA 4532 Silent System, article `9114276`, 500 mm.
- Spacer profile: Hettich article `13952`, 25 x 486 x 50 mm.
- Public manifest:
  `assets/hettich/ka-4532-spacer/hardware-assets.json`.
- Project-local source directories:

  ```text
  hardware/hettich/ka-4532-silent-system/9114276/source
  hardware/hettich/ka-4532-spacer-profile/13952/source
  ```

Both exact STEP files are required. Resolve them through
`$aikea-source-hardware-cad`; never scale another runner or replace the spacer
with invented geometry.

## Approved relationship

The spacer occupies 25 mm between each cabinet side and its 12.7 mm runner.
The wooden drawer therefore loses 37.7 mm of outside width on each side before
its own side thickness is considered. With 15 mm drawer sides, the generic
inside-width reduction is 105.4 mm.

The approved proof placed the runner centre 23 mm above the drawer bottom. The
runner begins 2 mm behind the drawer front. The spacer begins 10 mm behind the
drawer front and extends from 2 mm below the wooden drawer bottom to 48 mm above
it. These are saved construction observations, not client-project dimensions.

The left and right installations use the same unchanged spacer STEP. The right
instance is rigidly reoriented into the opposite cabinet-side frame; it is not
geometrically mirrored or scaled. The runner STEP contains both hands as four
native solids.
