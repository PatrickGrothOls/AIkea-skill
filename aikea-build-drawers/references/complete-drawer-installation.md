# Complete drawer installation

Every drawer includes its selected runner system and the machining needed to
install it. This applies to configurators, custom panel compositions, repeated
drawers and image-led designs. A wooden box is an intermediate calculation, not
a completed drawer. Recording missing runners as unresolved does not authorize
building and delivering a box-only drawer.

## Resolve the installation before generating the drawer

1. Read the actual opening, supporting panels, materials, door/hinge geometry,
   available depth, travel and intended load. Preserve exact saved product choices.
2. Select a compatible exact runner size and complete installation system before
   fixing the drawer's dimensions. Registered lengths are supported options, not
   universal defaults. Check width, depth, load and opening compatibility; do not
   force the furniture to fit a convenient existing profile.
3. Resolve verified source CAD and the installation specification, including both
   hands, companion parts, fixing positions, screws, and material-specific pilot
   diameter/depth. If data is missing, use
   [hardware sourcing](../../aikea-source-hardware-cad/SKILL.md). Obtain it with
   available tools, or give the exact product link, required file/input and visual
   download guidance for the user. Pause drawer generation until it is resolved.
   Inspecting sourced hardware separately is still useful; scaling another rail,
   inventing holes or substituting an unmachined box is not a resolution.
4. Resolve the clearance and any spacers below, then calculate the box from the
   remaining opening and the selected runner's installation dimensions. Use the
   same resolved dimensions for wood, hardware placements and drilling.
5. Build and verify one complete installation before repeating it. Each drawer
   needs its own complete runner set; a successful box calculator or geometry
   export does not establish that the selected generator supplies the installation.
   The [shared recipes](shared-drawer-construction.md) list current fixing gaps.

## Doors, hinges and spacers

Check the actual door at its usable opening angle, hinge arms and mounting plates
against the fixed runner envelope and the full travel of the drawer, its front
and moving runner members. Distinct fixing nodes alone do not prove clearance.

When door or hinge intrusion obstructs this installation or travel, include
compatible spacers automatically as part of the drawer installation. Derive the
needed offset from the actual obstruction and selected hardware's required
clearance; do not apply a universal spacer thickness. Keep a selected paired
runner/spacer profile together. A drawer with verified unobstructed travel does
not need spacers merely because another drawer does.

Include each required spacer as a real purchased or manufactured part with its
identity, dimensions, placement and attachment. Recalculate the clear opening,
box width, runner frames, screw engagement and host drilling together. Verify
the spacer-supported installation against the complete door/drawer movement.
If a compatible arrangement cannot be established, keep the drawer blocked and
resolve the specific constraint; do not silently omit the spacer or change the
client's selected hardware. Unknown hinge geometry is not proof of clearance.

Adding, changing or moving a door, hinge, runner or spacer invalidates the affected
drawer fit and machining evidence. Recheck the same complete assembly after the
change, including drawers that were built before the door.

## Machining and physical ownership

Represent the required mounting cuts on both host and drawer parts, plus spacer
attachments and companion fittings where required by the selected system.
Supplied screws still need their specified mounting preparation. Reconcile the
actual holes with the manufacturer's fixing pattern; neither a BOM entry nor
a visible runner solid proves those holes exist.

Plan all these cuts together with Cabineo joinery on each part's one chosen CNC
face. In particular, inner-face drawer joinery can conflict with outer-face
runner pilots. Resolve the construction without flipping the part or turning a
blind fixing into a through-hole just to pass the face audit. Follow the
[manufacturing process](../../aikea-design-furniture/references/manufacturing-process.md).

Keep each runner system owned once, with its fixed members on the host and its
moving members following the drawer through saved placements. Give every drawer
its own complete purchased set and record spacers and other required fittings in
the same physical inventory. Preserve the supplier's unit/pair and included-screw
packaging when counting purchases. Closed, open and exploded views must use that
same tree, including the hardware.

## Completion check

Reconcile every requested drawer with its installed runner set, required spacers,
actual mounting cuts and current fit/movement evidence. Declare installation and
machining requirements independently of the operations that satisfy them. Do not
delete missing requirements or relabel unresolved fixings as manual/nonmachining
work to obtain a pass. A general incomplete-preview allowance does not waive this
drawer contract.

Run the complete-tree operation and face checks, physical inventory and
[fabrication gate](../../aikea-review-unit/references/fabrication-readiness.md).
Missing hardware, holes, fixing data or clearance evidence keeps fabrication
blocked. A useful inspection artifact is not a CNC production package.
