# Fresh furniture build: reusable review lessons

The private room brief, machine paths, session history and artifact ledger have
been removed from this public document. Keep project measurements and run evidence
in an ignored project-local location; the original local record is preserved under
`local-evidence/privacy-cleanup/` in the cleanup worktree.

## Reusable findings

- Start a new design from its measured brief and current skills. Previous derived
  geometry is evidence, not a substitute for rebuilding the construction.
- Separate closed-pose clearance checks from full hinge and drawer travel checks.
  A visually convincing open-door illustration does not qualify a mechanism.
- Keep manufacturer CAD and source provenance distinct from inferred mounting
  datums, visual approximations and unqualified load assumptions.
- Match every presentation to its current inspection geometry. A successful bake,
  export or screenshot does not establish fabrication readiness.
- Preserve construction, fastening, tool-access and motion checks as separate
  completion gates. Surface unresolved qualifications explicitly.

lessons. No private room measurements or operational status are needed to use them.
